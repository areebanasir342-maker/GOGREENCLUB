About Us

Discover who we are and what we stand for.

Who Are We?

Bahria Earth & Space Society and Go Green Club are student-led platforms that encourage scientific curiosity, environmental responsibility, creativity and teamwork. We provide students with opportunities to learn, collaborate and make a positive impact.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote awareness about Earth, space science and environmental sustainability while empowering students to become responsible leaders and change-makers.

Our Objectives

  • Promote environmental awareness.
  • Encourage plantation and recycling.
  • Develop interest in Earth and Space sciences.
  • Organize educational events and campaigns.
  • Build leadership and teamwork skills.
  • Encourage sustainable lifestyles.

Our Hierarchy

Our organization operates through a structured hierarchy of mentors, presidents, vice presidents and departmental heads who work together to organize activities and achieve our goals.
